Our commitment to safety.

We are designing our aircraft to meet or exceed the rigorous safety standards for commercial aviation. We’re committed to keeping you safe, on the ground and in the air.

Autonomous flight with
human oversight.

Autonomous flight with human oversight.

With industry-leading autonomous technology and human oversight, our self-flying approach leverages the best of both worlds to increase safety.

Simplified design.

By simplifying our design, we have decreased the total number of safety-and flight-critical moving parts. Fewer moving parts means fewer potential safety issues.

Built to be failsafe.

Redundancy is built into every flight-critical system on Gen 6. From our failsafe battery and propulsion systems to our robust avionics and autonomy suite, every key feature has a backup, ensuring end-to-end safety at every stage of flight.
